Weather & Avionics

Weather History and Probability

Pulls historical NOAA weather for any U.S. airport and works out the odds of VFR, clear skies, or fog for whatever date range and time window you're planning around.

Know the Score(board)? – a Quiz

Develops and exhibits a solid understanding of how the Command Line (aka Scoreboard) is the critical check on your navigation and autopilot settings.

ATC

TEC Route Lookup

Looks up Tower Enroute Control routes straight from the FAA's own preferred-routes database — not simulator data — filtered by aircraft type and LAX's landing configuration.

Clearance Delivery Trainer

Call Clearance Delivery, hear your IFR clearance, copy it in CRAFT shorthand, then key Push to Talk and read it back — Clearance Delivery corrects anything you miss. Routes come from the FAA's preferred-route and TEC database plus recent real ATC clearances.

ATC Chair-Fly Trainer

Fires a random ATC instruction at you and times whether you set it up and read it back correctly — chair-flying practice for the Listen, Set, Read Back habit.

Time & Fuel Planning

Day's Flight Schedule

Plans a flying day of up to five legs — enter wheels-up time plus each leg's airports, ETE, and fuel, and it works out every clock time from leaving home to arriving destination. Or let Claude pull ETE and fuel from ForeFlight; saved days sync across your devices.

How Much Fuel to Add?

Enter the fuel you'll have after the current leg and the minimum you need for the next — alternate and reserve included — and it works out how much to add, in gallons and liters, total and per side.

Safety Stats

Piston GA Stats

Helps the owner/pilot compare two piston airplane types — Cirrus, Diamond, Cessna, Piper, Beech, Mooney — using NTSB accident records back to 1982: rates per 1,000 registered aircraft-years, accident category, phase of flight, severity, top findings, and the latest probable causes.

Checklists & Reference

Flying Yourself to Mexico?

A checklist for flying your own plane across the border — subscriptions and permits, eAPIS and flight-plan filings, what goes in the binder, and what to have onboard. Make a copy and customize it for your trip.
