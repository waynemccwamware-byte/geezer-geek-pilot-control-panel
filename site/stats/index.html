An “open” is one page load, including reloads and the author's own testing. Each app's counter started on a different date, so compare with that in mind.

“Visitors” counts distinct internet addresses (not people). Each app's row counts an address once for that app, so someone using three apps appears in three rows. The total at the top of the Visitors card counts each address once across all the apps, from 10-Nov-2026 when that shared count began; the Opened total adds up the rows.

Since 09-Nov-2026 every app counts on Tally. Fuel and Weather counted elsewhere before that: their opens carry the earlier count forward, but their visitors start from 09-Nov-2026. The Control Panel's opens include 300 estimated from before it had a counter.

“Per day” comes from a reading of every counter taken each night just after midnight Pacific time by the daily service check (history from 12-Nov-2026; the first day is partial). A day's bar is the increase between two readings. “New visitors” is the day's increase in distinct addresses: people seen for the first time, not everyone who visited that day. A faded bar covers less than a full day; a missed reading folds its days into the next bar.
